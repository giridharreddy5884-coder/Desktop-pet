## SCOOT
A guy who wanders around your screen
## What will he do
* He walks horizontally on the screen
* You can pick him up and put him somewhere else
* You can double click him to see him jump
* He tells you time
* He thanks you with a voice not everytime you drag and release him as a mark of remembering him
## How to get SCOOT
Download the exe from here:
